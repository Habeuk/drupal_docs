## Key Concepts

Throughout this guide, we will use terms such as **sections**, **layouts**, **buttons**, and more. It's important to understand these concepts from the start.

### Sections

A **section** represents a content block designed to highlight or display specific information. It spans the full width of the page. For example, on a homepage, you might have multiple sections:

- A section showcasing summaries of various services.
- A section displaying the latest blog posts.
- A section containing a contact form.
- And so on.

### Layouts

A **layout** refers to the graphical arrangement of elements such as titles, descriptions, images, icons, and more. In Drupal, a layout can range from a simple element like a button to a complex structure like a **section** or a **slider**.

#### Key Features of Layouts:

- **Flexibility**: Easily rearrange elements to suit your design needs.
- **Reusability**: Save and reuse layouts across multiple pages.
- **Customization**: Tailor layouts to match your brand identity.
